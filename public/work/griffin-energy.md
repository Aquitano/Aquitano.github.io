# Griffin Energy — Product Concept

**Year:** 2020  
**Scope:** Product design, frontend, and 3D modeling  
**Stack:** Blender, Photoshop, JavaScript, 3D

Griffin Energy is a fictional product concept developed from brand direction through a finished responsive website. Thomas Breindl designed the packaging in Photoshop, modeled and rendered a photorealistic can in Blender, and integrated those renders into an animated JavaScript site.
